# NeuroPeak Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 October 2026

NeuroPeak: IQ Arena (the "app") offers mini games and tests for mental
exercise and entertainment. This document explains what the app
collects, why, and how long it is kept.

In short: **there is no account, and we never ask for your name or
email.** The app knows you by a random identifier generated on your
device the first time you open it. That identifier exists only to keep
your own progress consistent; it is not tied to a person, a phone, or
an advertising identifier.

## What we collect

### 1. Stays on your device

The following is kept in your device's own storage. It is not sent to
any server and disappears when you delete the app.

- Your language preference
- Your onboarding answers (a copy may also go to the server, below)
- XP, level, stars, streak, daily tasks
- Brain map scores and the last seven days of activity
- League standing and weekly XP
- A local copy of your test results

### 2. Sent to the server

When the app is connected to a Supabase database, the following is
sent together with your random identifier:

| Data               | What it contains                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Why                                                      |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Onboarding answers | Age **range** (not a date of birth), focus areas, daily goal, the area you struggle with, exercise habit, notification permission                                                                                              | To build your daily plan and starting difficulty         |
| Test results       | Test id, raw score, IQ score, percentile, correct/answered counts, sub-dimension scores, duration, date                                                                                                                        | To show your history and compare with your age group     |
| Progress           | XP, level, current streak, longest streak, last active day, streak freezes left                                                                                                                                                | So your progress survives a change of device             |
| Usage events       | Event name (app open, test start/finish, game finish, paywall view, ad watched), the fields that come with it (game id, category, difficulty, level, test id, IQ score, correct count), platform (iOS/Android) and a timestamp | To see which games get played and where people get stuck |

None of these records contain a name, email, phone number, location,
contacts, photos, or a device identifier.

### 3. Third-party services

- **Google AdMob** — when ads are on. Google collects its own data to
  serve and measure ads (device type, approximate location,
  advertising identifier and similar). On iOS you are asked for
  tracking permission; if you decline, ads are not personalised and no
  feature of the app is withheld. Google's policy:
  <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **RevenueCat** — when subscriptions are on. Processes the
  subscription information that comes from the store to verify your
  purchase. <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — you buy through the store; we
  never see your card details.
- **Supabase** — the database provider that stores the data above.
  <https://supabase.com/privacy>

### 4. Notifications

We ask for notification permission during onboarding. **This release
does not send notifications and does not store your device's push
token.** When reminders are switched on, this document will be updated
and the date at the top will change.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share it with anyone other than the ad network above.
- We do not build a profile to advertise to you outside the app.
- This is not an app for children, and we do not knowingly collect
  data from anyone under 13.

## How long we keep it

Test results and progress are kept for at most 24 months after you
stop using the app. Usage events are deleted after 14 months. Data on
your device goes when you delete the app.

## Exercising your rights

Because there is no account, we find your data by your random
identifier. Write to <destek@neuropeak.app> to request a copy or
deletion; the **Settings → Email support** row in the app opens that
address. We answer within 30 days. If we do not know your random
identifier we may not be able to match the data — and if you have
already deleted the app, the identifier is gone with it.

## These tests are not a diagnosis

The IQ tests and measures such as "brain age" in this app are for
entertainment and mental exercise. They are not a clinical or
psychometric diagnostic tool and should not be used for any health
decision.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. For a
significant change we will also tell you inside the app.
