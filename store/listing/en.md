## Description (4000 characters)

```
Push your mind a little further every day.

NeuroPeak gives you 29 mini games and 7 IQ tests that train memory,
focus, logic, math and speed. Ten minutes a day is enough.

MEASURE
• The Basic IQ Test is free: 20 questions across number patterns,
  verbal relationships, logic and arithmetic.
• The result screen shows your score, your percentile and your verbal,
  numeric, visual and logical sub-dimensions.
• Advanced, Visual, Logical, Numerical, Verbal and Quick IQ tests
  unlock with PRO.

TRAIN
• Memory: Card Match, Light Sequence, Digit Span, N-Back, Grid Recall
• Attention: Colour Trap, Odd One Out, Symbol Hunt, Go/No-Go,
  Multi Tracking
• Logic: Matrix Puzzle, Liquid Sort, Logic Chain, One Line, Connect,
  Find the Way
• Math: Quick Math, Number Series, Target Number, Balance
• Spatial: Mental Rotation, Paper Fold, Shadow Match, Tiling
• Speed: Reaction, Quick Decision
• Verbal: Word Hunt, Word Analogy, Word Link

Every game has three difficulties, and the level rises as you do.

TRACK
• Your brain map shows current performance across five axes — not a
  measurement, a summary of your recent games.
• Daily tasks, streaks and levels make it easy to come back.
• Compete in a weekly league of 30; the top 10 move up a division.
• Badges mark how many rounds you have played and how far you got.

NEUROPEAK PRO
• Every IQ and personality test
• A detailed report and plan after every test
• No ads
• Unlimited hints and stars
• Brain map history and weekly trend

The subscription is weekly or annual. The price appears on the
purchase screen in your own currency. It renews automatically unless
cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; cancel in your App
Store settings.

TWO LANGUAGES
English and Turkish. The word bank behind the verbal games was written
separately for each language — not translated.

IMPORTANT
NeuroPeak tests are for entertainment and mental exercise. They are
not a clinical or psychometric diagnostic tool and should not be used
for any health decision.

Privacy: https://neuropeak.app/privacy
Terms: https://neuropeak.app/terms
Support: destek@neuropeak.app
```

## Age rating

Expected: **12+**. Every category in Apple's questionnaire answers
"None"; the ads checkbox is ticked because the app can show ads. A 4+
rating would also require pinning the ad network's content rating
to 4+.

## To keep true

The numbers in the description come from the code: 29 games in
`GAME_CATALOGUE`, 6 tests in `CONTENT_TESTS`. If the catalogue
changes, this text has to change with it.
