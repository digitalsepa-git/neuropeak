NeuroPeak Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

NeuroPeak: IQ Arena (the "app") offers mini games and tests for mental exercise and entertainment. This document explains what the app collects, why, and how long it is kept.

In short: there is no account, and we never ask for your name or email. The app knows you by a random identifier generated on your device the first time you open it. That identifier exists only to keep your own progress consistent; it is not tied to a person, a phone, or an advertising identifier.

What we collect

1. Stays on your device

The following is kept in your device's own storage. It is not sent to any server and disappears when you delete the app.

  • Your language preference
  • Your onboarding answers (a copy may also go to the server, below)
  • XP, level, stars, streak, daily tasks
  • Brain map scores and the last seven days of activity
  • League standing and weekly XP
  • A local copy of your test results

2. Sent to the server

When the app is connected to a Supabase database, the following is sent together with your random identifier:

Data What it contains Why
Push token If you turned notifications on: your device's push token, platform and language To send reminders
Onboarding answers Age range (not a date of birth), focus areas, daily goal, the area you struggle with, exercise habit, notification permission To build your daily plan and starting difficulty
Test results Test id, raw score, IQ score, percentile, correct/answered counts, sub-dimension scores, duration, date To show your history and compare with your age group
Progress XP, level, current streak, longest streak, last active day, streak freezes left So your progress survives a change of device
Usage events Event name (app open, test start/finish, game finish, paywall view, ad watched), the fields that come with it (game id, category, difficulty, level, test id, IQ score, correct count), platform (iOS/Android) and a timestamp To see which games get played and where people get stuck

None of these records contain a name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or a device identifier.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — when ads are on. Google collects its own data to serve and measure ads (device type, approximate location, advertising identifier and similar). On iOS you are asked for tracking permission; if you decline, ads are not personalised and no feature of the app is withheld. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — when subscriptions are on. Processes the subscription information that comes from the store to verify your purchase. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — you buy through the store; we never see your card details.
  • Supabase — the database provider that stores the data above. https://supabase.com/privacy

4. Notifications

Notifications are off by default. When you turn them on in Settings — and only then — your device is asked for notification permission, and if you grant it your device's push token is stored on our server. What is stored is a string that addresses the device; it contains neither your name, nor your email, nor your location.

When you turn notifications off the record is not deleted but deactivated, so the same device is recognised if you turn them back on. If you want the record gone entirely, deleting your account removes the push token with it.

This release does not send notifications yet: the token is stored but the sending infrastructure is not in place. When sending begins, this document will be updated and the date at the top will change.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with anyone other than the ad network above.
  • We do not build a profile to advertise to you outside the app.
  • This is not an app for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

How long we keep it

Test results and progress are kept for at most 24 months after you stop using the app. Usage events are deleted after 14 months. Data on your device goes when you delete the app.

Exercising your rights

Because there is no account, we find your data by your random identifier. Write to destek@neuropeak.app to request a copy or deletion; the Settings → Email support row in the app opens that address. We answer within 30 days. If we do not know your random identifier we may not be able to match the data — and if you have already deleted the app, the identifier is gone with it.

These tests are not a diagnosis

The IQ tests and measures such as "brain age" in this app are for entertainment and mental exercise. They are not a clinical or psychometric diagnostic tool and should not be used for any health decision.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. For a significant change we will also tell you inside the app.
